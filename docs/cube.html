🟢 Beginner Method (Layer by Layer)

The easiest method to learn. Solves the cube layer by layer: Cross → Corners → Middle Layer → Yellow Cross → Yellow Edges → Yellow Corners → Orient Corners.

White Cross

Intuitive - place 4 white edges around white center matching side centers

White Corners

R U R' U'

Repeat until corner is solved. Hold cube with corner at URF.

Middle Layer Edges

U R U' R' U' F' U F

For edge going right. Mirror: U' L' U L U F U' F' for left.

Yellow Cross

F R U R' U' F'

Dot → Line → L-shape → Cross. Repeat as needed.

Yellow Edges

R U R' U R U2 R' U

Align 2 edges, hold them at back/left, then apply.

Yellow Corners Position

U R U' L' U R' U' L

Cycle 3 corners. Repeat until all in correct spots.

Orient Yellow Corners

R' D' R D

Hold unsolved corner at URF. Repeat 2-4 times. Then U to next.

🔵 CFOP (Fridrich) - Speedcubing Standard

Cross → F2L (First 2 Layers) → OLL (Orient Last Layer) → PLL (Permute Last Layer). Used by most world record holders.

F2L - First Two Layers

41 cases. Solve corner-edge pairs and insert. Key cases:

Case 1 - Basic Pair

R U R'

Case 2 - Separated

U R U' R'

Case 3 - Top Pair

R U2 R' U' R U R'

🟣 Roux Method

Block building method: First Block → Second Block → CMLL → LSE (Last Six Edges). Fewer algorithms, intuitive.

First Block (FB)

Build 1x2x3 block on left (DL, DFL, DLB, FL, BL, UL). Intuitive.

Second Block (SB)

Build 1x2x3 on right (DR, DFR, DRB, FR, BR, UR). Intuitive.

CMLL (46 algs)

R U2 R' U' R U R' U' R U' R'

Orient + permute last layer corners. Example: Sune.

LSE (Last Six Edges)

M U M' U2 M' U M'

Orient edges → permute edges with M moves only.

🟠 ZZ Method

EOLine → F2L → LL. Edge orientation first means no F/B moves in F2L, simpler last layer.

EOLine

F (R U R' U') F'

Orient all edges while solving DF/DB. Then F2L with only R/U/L/D.

ZZ F2L

Only R, U, L, D moves. No cube rotations needed.

ZBLL (493 algs)

Solve last layer in 1 look. Most use OCLL+PLL subset.

🔴 R2 Method

Uses mostly R2, U, and f moves. Good for one-handed or fewest moves.

Cross + EO

R2 U R2 U' R2

Corner Position

R2 U R2 U2 R2

Parity Fix

r U2 r' U2 r' F r F'
