How the AI Thinks

The Minimax algorithm explores all possible game states to find the optimal move:

  • Max player (AI) tries to maximize score
  • Min player (Human) tries to minimize score
  • Win = +10, Loss = -10, Draw = 0
  • Recursively evaluates all future positions

On Hard difficulty, the AI plays perfectly - it cannot lose. On Medium, it sometimes makes suboptimal moves. On Easy, it plays randomly.
